Tissue Presets

Each preset value is the value of one row of the literature table below, and the cell shows that source. Values from different sources are not averaged, so a preset is one published result and not a consensus value. A value with the mark "illustrative" has no source: no readable published value was found for it. "Read once" and "from rate" have the same meaning as the marks of the literature table. The brain presets are for human brain in vivo. Note: Tissues vary significantly by age, region, and pathology.

Literature Values

Each row is one published measurement, with its reference. The table has brain tissue of human, mouse and rat, and also human brain lesions, fat, bone marrow, muscle and blood (see the "Tissue type" filter). Values are shown with the ± error as given in the source (usually an SD; see the notes of the row). Rows from different sources are not averaged, and they can disagree: the result depends on the method, the region, the age and, for ex vivo tissue, on the fixation, the temperature and contrast agents. A dash shows that the source does not give the value.

Contrast Agent Relaxivities

Published relaxivities of gadolinium contrast agents, in 1/(mM·s). The relaxivity depends on the agent, the field strength, the medium and the temperature. The largest source of r1 and r2 is Rohrer et al. 2005 (water, bovine plasma and canine blood). Values in human plasma and human blood are from other papers, and many of them give only r1. A row with the mark "Cited value" has a value that its paper did not measure. A button sets the relaxivities of the Contrast agent option. The default relaxivities of the simulator are those of gadoterate in bovine plasma at 1.5 T (Rohrer et al. 2005).
